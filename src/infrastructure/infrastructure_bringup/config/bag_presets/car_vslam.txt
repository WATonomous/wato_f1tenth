# Car recording preset for REALSENSE_MODE=vslam (Isaac ROS Visual SLAM): ./watod bag record
# (the default preset in vslam mode) or ./watod bag record --preset car_vslam
#
# The car preset with the RealSense infrared stereo pair instead of color + depth: both infrared
# images lossless (zstd), ~60 Hz each, with their camera_info. The IMU is /hal/imu with
# IMU=realsense, else /camera/camera/imu (both listed); the camera TFs are in /tf_static.
#
# Left out on purpose: /pf/viz/* (costs the particle filter its 40 Hz floor), raw images
# (~1 MB per frame through DDS), and the RealSense metadata topics.
